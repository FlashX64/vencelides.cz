- `layouts/partials/header.html`
- `layouts/partials/head.html`
- `layouts/partials/base-foot.html`
- `layouts/partials/head-favicons.html`
- `layouts/partials/seo/breadcrumbs-jsonld.html`
- `assets/scss/_foundation.scss` — base typography
- `assets/scss/_navigation-layout.scss` — header layout
- `assets/scss/_page-transition.scss`
