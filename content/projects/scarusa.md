## Character design

Scarusa is a strong, independent survivor with a bold tomboy personality. Inspired by Medusa from Greek mythology, she is reimagined as a tough wanderer adapted to a dangerous world. Despite her monstrous appearance, the design retains elements of femininity and sexuality.

## Process

{{< article-panel kind="features" title="Modeling and texturing" >}}
- **Modeling**
  The character was first sculpted and then manually retopologized.
- **Normal maps**
  I baked the normal maps from the high-poly model in Substance Painter. The exception is the snake-skin normal map for the lower body, which was generated using ChatGPT.
- **Albedo textures**
  I hand-painted the albedo textures in Blender.
