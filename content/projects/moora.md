A 3D model based on my original concept, created as the final project for the 3D Modeling course at Masaryk University.

Moora is a sci-fi robot inspired by nature, designed to help restore ecosystems affected by long-term ecological decline. Set in a world where once-healthy landscapes have turned into fragile moorlands, its purpose is to monitor environmental conditions and support the recovery of natural balance.

## Design
Moora combines a moth-like body shape with high-visibility caution coloring inspired by nature. Its functional systems support the robot's role in environmental monitoring and restoration.

{{< article-panel kind="features" title="Functional systems" >}}
- **Soil-contact sensor**
  Measures moisture, pH, and nutrient levels
- **Optical system**
  Proximity scanning and infrared imaging for leaf health and water stress
- **Watering module**
  Integrated nozzle and storage tank
- **Wing-mounted sensors**
  Solar energy collection and environmental data gathering
- **GPS antennas**
  Navigation
