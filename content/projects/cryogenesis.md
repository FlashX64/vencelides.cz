A first-person rail shooter developed as part of the Game Development course at Masaryk University in a team of three together with Mikuláš Šťastný and Adam Renčo. The initial prototype was also co-developed with Jo Hyorim.

The game is set deep within a classified underground research facility overrun by a cryogenic parasite. You play as a maintenance worker fighting through infected creatures and former co-workers to survive.

## Finished game

{{< article-panel kind="features" title="My contribution" >}}

- **Camera and gameplay events**
  I implemented the camera system and the event scheduling system that drives gameplay events along the camera path.
- **Art direction and environments**
  I led most of the art direction and level design. I modeled and textured all 3D assets, including the level environments, except the zombie enemy. I used AI-generated textures as a starting point, then manually refined them, adjusted their colors, added details, and reduced their resolution to match the game's retro aesthetic.
